Ocean's Dread is a virtual reality horror experience set within an expansive underwater maze of stone-like tunnels. As players swim deeper, the environment shifts around them in size and texture: narrow passages press in and heighten the sense of confinement, while sudden openings offer brief moments of freedom. These changes shape the player's emotional journey, building waves of anxiety and relief as they push further into the dark.

The tunnels are built from contrasting materials, from rough, jagged stone to smooth, worn surfaces, each altering the sensory feel of the space and drawing players onward to uncover what lies ahead. Subtle, dynamic shifts in lighting, sound, and environment pull players ever deeper into this eerie underwater world.
<br>

## Every stroke costs oxygen

Rather than gliding through the world with a thumbstick, players move by physically swimming. Hand gestures, combined with the VR controllers, mimic real swimming motions, letting players propel themselves through the caves in a natural and intuitive way. This embodied interaction places the body at the centre of the experience, deepening the sense of presence and making every movement feel deliberate.

Survival depends on careful strategy. Players must manage a limited oxygen supply while navigating a complex, ever-changing maze, and the tension grows with each unfamiliar turn. Dark tunnels and an unsettling atmosphere turn exploration into a psychological challenge, making the game as mentally demanding as it is physical.

<!-- ![Sub Image](/assets/Images/Work/OceansDread/OD_Gameplay.png) -->

Beyond the thrill of horror, Ocean's Dread explores how games can offer a safe, virtual space for confronting fear and anxiety. It is designed for players who enjoy problem-solving, horror-themed gameplay, and unconventional interaction design, appealing to VR enthusiasts, fans of adventure and survival games, and anyone seeking a psychologically engaging experience.

Ocean's Dread is currently a work in progress. The core swimming mechanics and underwater environment are implemented and playable, with ongoing development focused on refining the atmosphere, introducing new obstacles and gameplay dynamics, and adding varying levels of difficulty, all shaped by playtesting and player feedback.
